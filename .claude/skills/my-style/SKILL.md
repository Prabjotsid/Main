# my-style

Format: 9:16 Instagram Reels (1080x1920, 30 fps), 30-45 s, one idea per reel,
60 s at most. Learned from 6 reference reels (see `references/`) and from
what Instagram's head has said ranks Reels: watch time, sends, likes.
Version 1, 17 Nov 2026. Not yet used on a real edit: expect to adjust numbers
after the first exports.

Who decided what:

- Measured habits come from the references (numbers in `references/`).
- The owner set the silence rule (0.1 s) themselves.
- Brand, direction, length, caption coverage and music were **chosen by
  Claude on 17 Nov 2026 because the owner asked it to decide**. They are
  starting points the owner can change at any time, not facts about an
  existing brand. If the owner states a font, colour or logo, theirs
  replaces these.

## Brand

| Item | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Direction | "Ember": warm, dark, cinematic | from the brand sheet, direction A |
| Display font (hook, big words) | Instrument Serif, italic for the one big word | free, Google Fonts (OFL) |
| Caption font | Inter Tight, weight 700 | free, Google Fonts (OFL) |
| Text colour | `#F4E9D8` cream | not pure white |
| Accent (one emphasised word) | `#E0A458` amber | the references use no colour accent; this is the owner's own mark |
| Shade behind text | `#14110F` at 35-45 % opacity, soft, only when the picture is bright | no hard box, no outline |
| Logo | NOT SET - ask the owner | no logo or handle goes on screen until the owner gives one |

## Rules

Each rule is "do X when Y". Brackets give the range seen and how many
references support it; "choice" marks a decision rather than a measurement.

### Hook (first 3 s)
- Put the hook text on screen from the first frame when the reel opens. (3 of 6)
- Start speaking at 0.0 s when the reel has a voice; say at most 10 words in the first 3 s. (0.0 s in 2 of 3; 10-11 words in 2 of 3)
- Hold the opening picture and make the first cut between 1.5 and 3 s. (first cut 1.7-3.5 s in 4 of 6)
- Open on the strongest line or picture of the whole reel, not on a greeting, when choosing what goes first. (choice: watch time is the top ranking signal)

### Cuts and silences
- Cut every silence longer than 0.1 s when the owner is speaking. (owner's rule; the references trim to 0.38-0.42 s) If speech sounds clipped, tell the owner and offer 0.2 s; do not change it silently.
- Use hard cuts only; allow one soft transition at the very end. (hard cuts 53 of 56, 95 %)
- Change the picture at least every 2 to 3 s when one person is talking: a new angle, B-roll, a text card or a re-frame. (shots 2.4-2.6 s median, 3 of 6)
- Allow one fast burst per reel of 3 clips at about 0.8 s each when showing examples. (1 of 6)

### Zooms
- Add a slow push-in of 6-10 % across a whole shot on about 1 shot in 3 when the shot is otherwise still. (10 % over 7.5 s, 1 of 6)
- Re-frame by cutting to a tighter crop (about 1.15x) on a new sentence when there is only one camera angle, instead of zooming on single words. (choice; no reference punches in on words)

### Captions and text
- Caption every spoken word, in the language spoken, 1-3 words at a time, in step with the voice. (1-3 words per line, 3 of 4; every word is a choice for viewers with sound off)
- Set captions in the caption font at about 3.5 % of frame height, centred, with their centre 62 % down the frame. (choice: the references sit at 51 % and 12-35 %; 62 % keeps clear of the face and of Instagram's buttons)
- Colour one word per caption in the accent colour when that word carries the point. (choice)
- Set the hook and section titles as a stacked line in the display font: one word at about 8 % of frame height, the rest at about 2.5-3 %, in the upper third (centre 18-30 % from the top). (2 of 4)
- Bring title words in one at a time, about 3 words per second. (5 words in 1.5 s, 1 of 4)
- Show at most 6 words of title and one caption group at once. (6-7 max, 2 of 4)
- Keep text out of the top 12 %, the bottom 30 % and the right 10 % of the frame. (choice, from published safe-zone guides, which disagree by a few percent; preview on a phone)
- Use no outlines, boxes, emojis or stickers. (0 of 6 use them)

### Motion graphics and B-roll
- Show a phone or screen mock-up on a plain dark background when explaining something that happens on a screen. (2 of 2 tutorials)
- Cut to B-roll or a text card when the owner names a thing that can be shown. (choice)
- Keep every graphic in the brand colours and fonts; add nothing that moves without a reason.

### Colour and look
- Grade warm when finishing any shot. (warm tilt +0.02 to +0.34, 6 of 6)
- Cap whites at about 85 % and keep blacks at 3-5 % when grading. (whites 63-94 %, blacks 0-13 %)
- Keep faces natural and readable; do not go as dark as the moodiest references when someone is talking. (choice: those run at 3-19 % brightness with no face on screen)
- Keep one look for the whole reel.

### Sound
- Master to about -14 LUFS with true peak under -1 dBTP. (-14.6 to -14.1, 5 of 5; one reference clips at +0.2 and is not to be copied)
- Keep loudness even from start to end. (range 1.1-3.5 LU)
- Keep any music clearly under the voice, about 16-20 dB below it, when someone is speaking. (choice; the references' balance could not be measured)
- Add a sound effect only when something visibly happens. (effects on cuts were not above chance in 4 of 5)
- Music: by default export without a music track and have the owner add a sound inside Instagram, because no licensed track has been provided. Use a track in the export only when the owner supplies one or approves a free one with its licence written down. (choice)

### Ending
- End on the pay-off line and stop; cut any goodbye. Finish within 45 s when one idea is covered. (choice)
- Make the last second flow back into the first when it can be done naturally. (choice: loops add watch time)

## Not from the references
- Their wording, hooks, logos, handles, footage, music tracks and sound
  effect files are not part of this style. The fast "walking" montage is not
  part of it either; it needs footage shot for it.

## Least certain
- Caption sizes and positions were read by eye from small frames.
- Voice-to-music balance and which sounds are effects could not be measured.
- The six references are four kinds of video; several rules rest on one or
  two of them. Nothing here has been proven to make a reel go viral.
