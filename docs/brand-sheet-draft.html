Findings

What the references have in common

Cuts and silences

  • Almost every cut is a plain hard cut. Dissolves and wipes are rare.
  • In the three calm, cinematic reels a shot lasts about two and a half seconds.
  • When someone speaks, they speak fast and the gaps are trimmed to under half a second.
  • The montage is the exception: it holds still for 3 seconds, then cuts about three times a second.

Captions and on-screen text

  • Text is white with no outline, no box and no coloured highlight word. Emphasis comes from size.
  • Two reels use a high-contrast display serif, stacked in mixed sizes: one huge word, small words tucked around it. Words arrive one at a time and each shot gets a new stack.
  • One reel shows a single small lowercase word at a time, dead centre, under a heavy slanted capital title.
  • Lines are short: one to three words. No emojis anywhere.

Proposal

Draft style for you

These rules take the habits above and set them as your own numbers. Fonts, colours and logo are left open on purpose: those are yours to give, and I won't fill them in by guessing.

Hook

  • Put your text on screen from the first frame.
  • Hold the opening picture; make the first cut between 1.5 and 3 s.
  • If you speak, start at 0.0 s and say no more than 10 words in the first 3 s.

Cuts

  • Use hard cuts only. Save one soft transition for the very end.
  • Hold each shot 2 to 3 s when the mood is calm.
  • Allow one fast burst per reel: 3 clips of about 0.8 s each.
  • Trim every pause in speech to 0.4 s or less.

Zooms

  • Use a slow push-in of 6 to 10% across a whole shot, on about 1 shot in 3.
  • Do not punch in on words.

Text

  • One idea per shot, 6 words at most, 1 to 3 words per line.
  • Make one word large (about 8% of frame height) and the rest small (about 2.5%).
  • Bring words in one at a time, in step with the voice or the beat.
  • Keep the top 12% and bottom 20% of the frame free of text, where Instagram puts its own buttons.
  • No outlines, boxes, emojis or stickers.

Colour

  • Grade warm.
  • Cap the whites at about 85% brightness and keep blacks near 3 to 5%.
  • Keep one look for the whole reel.

Sound

  • Master to −14 LUFS with peaks under −1 dBTP.
  • Let music lead; keep volume even from start to end.
  • Add an effect only where something visibly happens.

Before I finalize

What I need from you

  1. What are your reels about, and what is in your raw footage? Talking to camera, scenery with music, or both? The references split both ways.
  2. Which direction: A, B, C, or a mix? Say what you'd change.
  3. Your brand: fonts, colours as hex codes, and a logo file, if you have them. If you don't, say so and I'll offer choices for you to pick from.
  4. Captions: which language, and do you want every spoken word captioned or only key lines?
  5. Length: the references run 7.5 to 89 s. Earlier you mentioned 45 to 60 s. Which do you want?
  6. Music: will you add a trending sound inside Instagram, or should the exported video carry its own music? If its own, I need a track you have the rights to.
  7. The fast walking montage: do you want that as a format too? It needs footage shot for it, so I've left it out of the rules.
Least certain

Three things to double-check

  • Caption sizes and positions were read by eye from small frames, so treat them as within a few percent.
  • Sound effects and voice-to-music balance could not be measured reliably. Only overall loudness is solid.
  • The sample is small and mixed. Some points rest on one or two reels. More references of the one kind you want would firm up the numbers.
